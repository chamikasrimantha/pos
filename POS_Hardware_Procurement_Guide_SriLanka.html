6. Technical Compatibility & Best Practices

  1. Cash Drawer Connection: The cash drawer does not connect to the computer's USB port. It plugs directly into the RJ11 port (marked "DK") on the back of the 80mm receipt printer. When a cash invoice completes, the printer sends a 24V electrical pulse to pop the drawer open automatically.
  2. Label Printer Auto-Calibration: When loading 50×30mm sticker rolls into the Xprinter XP-365B, hold the feed button until it beeps twice. The printer automatically calibrates the 2mm gap between stickers. Set Windows paper size to 50mm Width × 30mm Height, 0mm Margin.
  3. Zero Additional Cost for Mobile Stock Counting: Staff and managers do not need to purchase expensive wireless handheld terminals for stocktaking or inter-branch transfers. The system features a built-in camera barcode scanner that runs directly on any Android or iPhone smartphone via Google Chrome or Safari.
  4. Redundant Internet: Set up a secondary 4G Wi-Fi SIM router (Dialog / SLT-Mobitel) at both counters so billing never stops if the primary fiber connection drops.
